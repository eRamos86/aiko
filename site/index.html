how she works

You talk to Aiko. Aiko talks to everything else.

🐾 chat: plain conversation with her brain of choice. She keeps history, switches models mid-chat, and answers in-character.

🐾 dispatch: "there's a login bug in flux" → she decomposes, picks a target (local for quick stuff, server for heavy jobs), launches the right agent, watches it, and reports back.

🐾 plan: /plan — dump your thoughts, she organizes them into tasks, doc destinations, and open questions. Nothing gets dispatched until you say so.
